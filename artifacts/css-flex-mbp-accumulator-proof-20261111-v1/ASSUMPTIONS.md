# Exact boundary

The approved `Acquired` predicate supplies only list length≤UINT32_MAX and each MBP contribution in the full signed32 range. It does not supply prefix/final wide safety. `prefix_target` derives those ranges, including the asymmetric minimum −2^31.

The accumulator executes a mathematical checked fold. Success means each checked intermediate is representable; failure gives no native C++ value. Wide success is derived universally on Acquired. The three old failure boundaries prove failure of checked32 and success of checked64 on the same inputs; the old native executable is not recompiled or executed here.

The generic free-space theorem assumes already acquired inner/gap/used-main operands and representability of the occupied-space sum and subtraction prefixes, exactly as the frozen DownstreamFits predicate states. It proves the native-shaped order of addition and subtraction yields the exact mathematical expression. It does not prove SumOfGaps, grow/shrink selection, early freezing or subsequent allocation/rounding. For the compensated symbolic family those input-domain and downstream conditions are conclusions, so the concrete bug companion does not rely on an uninhabited downstream assumption.

The source mapping is inherited from the frozen arithmetic assessment: `mTotalItemMBP` starts at zero, accumulates each already computed MBP, then enters the occupied-space/free-space expression. This is a manually reviewed source-shaped model of the proposed one-field widening, not mechanically extracted C++, LLVM or machine code. Normalizing individual margin/border/padding arithmetic and preserving native storage remain separate obligations.

The compiled control functions are intentionally wrong Lean implementations. Their concrete wrong-result proofs and negated claims test semantic sensitivity.
