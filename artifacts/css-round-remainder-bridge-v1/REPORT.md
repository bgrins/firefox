# Exact remainder to directed-rounding bridge

Six small support results compile in a standalone research module: four algorithm results and two rational-transfer results. No canonical files or audit counts are changed. The new obligation differs from the existing CssIntegerRounding unit-integer interval/floor model: it connects a truncating remainder contract for an arbitrary positive step to directed adjacent multiples and global nearest selection. Existing Rat/Int arithmetic/order lemmas are reused. CANONICAL-AUDIT.json records the bounded model inventory and exact files read.

Represent finite rational inputs on a common positive denominator: value=a/d, step=b/d, remainder=r/d. The contract states b>0, a=q*b+r, −b<r<b and remainder sign consistent with a. q is a mathematical integer witness, not a quotient obtained by floating division. For nonzero remainder, remainder_bounds constructs consecutive integer multiples strictly enclosing a. adjacent_directed proves the lower/upper values are respectively the greatest multiple below a and least multiple above a. nearest_minimal_upper_tie proves the selected multiple is globally distance-minimal against every integer multiple and resolves equal distances toward the larger result. remainder_toward_zero proves the magnitude comparison selects a−r. Positive-denominator order and rational_nearest_refinement transfer the distance comparison to exact rational values.

Exact anonymous witnesses instantiate ±33554432 with step11184811 and remainders±11184810 (shared denominator33554432), yielding the two observed directed bounds. They also establish that the input numerator is not itself a step multiple and that its positive quotient lies just below3. The supplied rounded quotient3 therefore cannot identify the lower multiple. This corroborates the source-first/browser-observed witness; it is not a second bug discovery or an additional counted generic theorem. Positive and negative nearest ties are checked. Three deliberately false controls reject dropping the remainder-sign bound, dropping the remainder-magnitude bound, or choosing the lower nearest tie.

The model is conditional exact arithmetic. It does not identify Rust % with SMT-LIB fp.rem: the latter's quotient convention differs. Native remainder exactness, f64 reconstruction and distance error, final f32 projection, signed-zero encoding, NaN/infinity handling, CSS context/type rules and compiler correctness remain separate contracts. Rat collapses both zero signs. The actual zero-remainder branch's input preservation is inspected and its exact multiple identity is checked, but signed-bit preservation is covered by the separate extracted helper/browser controls, not this model.

The proposed exponent-gap split for future IEEE work is not assumed here. Bounds smaller than a half-ULP might remain in one f32 rounding cell, while a sufficiently large step might limit significant-bit span, but binade/subnormal boundaries and ties still need proof. No universal exact f64 addition claim is made. The independently reviewed finite helper evidence remains useful corroboration, not that missing universal proof.

Validation uses the existing pinned Lean4.35.0-rc4 executable with LEAN_NUM_THREADS=2 and a55-second limit per invocation; no new Lake target, cache or compiler product. replay.py writes only a fresh reviewer output directory and owned temporary negative-source files, which are removed.
