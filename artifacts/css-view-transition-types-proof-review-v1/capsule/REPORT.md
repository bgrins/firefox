# Initial view-transition type representation

Producer companion for the accepted initial-type deduplication repair. It preserves the native review's promised initialization, successful mutation and direct-observation scope. It is not a proof of all View Transitions, style invalidation or native memory safety.

## Results

90 named Lean declarations are audited across six new modules. They cover a first-occurrence ordered-set reference; source-shaped seen/vector initialization for every finite prefix; uniqueness, order and complete membership; owning-reference availability for weak keys; successful lazy backing initialization; arbitrary finite Add/Delete/Clear/Observe histories and replies; exact UTF-16/atom identity transport; a live-generation atom model allowing deletion/reallocation; and owner/root/state/valid-name-qualified direct selector observations. Seventeen boundary witnesses plus two live-generation results are included in the count. General results are conditional on their explicit provider premises, not finite-case induction guesses.

The native initialization and public mutation/selector control paths are extracted from retained source into a standalone C++ harness with documented substituted providers. The candidate, Lean driver and independent Python ordered-set oracle agree on 1,755 cases: 21 historical WPT fixtures, 1,720 short histories, 12 arbitrary-code-unit histories and two absent-option branches. Original source extraction reproduces the five observable duplicate-deletion disagreements from the accepted browser pair. Initial duplicate vector contents alone are not counted as a public observable discrepancy. Two compiled control mutants distinguish reversed initial order and omitted native deletion. Three additional Lean semantic mutants must fail their proofs.

The full frozen replay is the acceptance gate; development logs alone are not acceptance. Independent review must follow freezing.

## Unresolved scope

Ignored initialization errors, OOM/GC, general JS conversion/realm behavior, live iterators, asynchronous lifecycle and scheduling, cross-document/scoped transitions, invalid/reserved selector policy, stale completed-transition interference and restyle invalidation remain outside this repair's accepted native scope. The separate scout's invalidation milestone remains open. Public set membership and direct matches do not prove computed-style invalidation.
